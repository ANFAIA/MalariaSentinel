# Water vapour is a pre-oviposition attractant for the malaria vector Anopheles gambiae sensu stricto

**Authors:** Michael N. Okal, Benjamin Francis, Manuela Herrera-Varela, Ulrike Fillinger, Steven W. Lindsay
**Journal:** Malaria Journal | **Year:** 2013 | **DOI:** 10.1186/1475-2875-12-365
**File:** papers/anopheles-dynamics/okal-2013-water-vapour-oviposition.md

---

## Abstract (summary)

Semi-field experiments tested whether gravid *Anopheles gambiae* s.s. detect
and respond to water vapour as a pre-oviposition cue. Water vapour alone
attracted gravid females, establishing it as a major chemical signal from a
potential larval habitat.

## Key Results (as used by the model)

- Gravid females locate water through a short-range water-vapour plume.
- The engine models this as `oviposition.scale_m`, the decay scale of the
  oviposition-site plume.

## Relevance to MalariaSentinel (Centinela)

Provenance for the oviposition attraction kernel. The model uses the plume
shape, not the paper's trap-catch rates.
