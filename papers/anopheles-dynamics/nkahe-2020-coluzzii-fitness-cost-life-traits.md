# Fitness cost of insecticide resistance on the life-traits of a Anopheles coluzzii population from the city of Yaoundé, Cameroon

**Authors:** Diane Leslie Nkahe, Edmond Kopya, Borel Djiappi-Tchamen, Wilson Toussile, Nadege Sonhafouo-Chiana, Sevilor Kekeunou, Remy Mimpfoundi, Parfait Awono-Ambene, Charles Sinclair Wondji, Christophe Antonio-Nkondjio
**Journal:** Wellcome Open Research | **Year:** 2020 (5) | **DOI:** 10.12688/wellcomeopenres.16039.2
**File:** papers/anopheles-dynamics/nkahe-2020-coluzzii-fitness-cost-life-traits.md

---

## Abstract (summary)

Comparison of life-history traits of a **susceptible and a pyrethroid-resistant
colony of *Anopheles coluzzii*** from Yaoundé, Cameroon: blood-feeding rate,
fecundity and fertility, larval development time, pupation, emergence, sex
ratio, adult longevity. *An. gambiae* s.l. were also reared from the same
generation but only *An. coluzzii* was maintained in the insectarium across
generations.

## Key Results (as used by the model)

- **Fecundity (*A. coluzzii*): susceptible colony 111.67 ± 5.36 eggs/female
  (N = 9604 eggs), resistant colony 93.33 ± 10.77 eggs/female (N = 3736
  eggs)** (Kruskal–Wallis χ² = 42.86, df = 1, P = 5.89 × 10⁻¹¹).
- **Fertility (hatching rate):** 73.94 % (7101/9604) in susceptible vs
  65.82 % (2459/3736) in resistant (P < 0.0001).
- **Larval development time: 7.57 ± 0.18 days (susceptible) vs
  10.61 ± 0.17 days (resistant)** — a 3-day penalty under resistance.
- **Pupation:** 63.19 ± 4.37 % vs 49.40 ± 7.70 % (P = 0.12, not significant);
  **emergence** 54.26 ± 6.76 % vs 47.68 ± 7.48 % (P = 0.74).
- **Adult live span: females 21.71 ± 0.47 days (susceptible) vs
  13.90 ± 0.46 days (resistant); males 20.77 ± 0.52 vs 15.35 ± 0.41 days.**
- Blood-feeding rate 93.33 % (susceptible) vs 34 % (resistant),
  P < 0.0001; sex ratio biased in the resistant colony (28.10 % females vs
  23.13 %, P = 0.032).
- The authors note that the extrinsic incubation period of *P. falciparum* is
  ~12 days, so the shortened lifespan of resistant coluzzii directly limits
  their vectorial capacity.

## Relevance to MalariaSentinel (Centinela)

The best available **species-specific fecundity and life-traits data for
*A. coluzzii***, and therefore the provenance source for the egg-batch
parameters of the engine (`egg_batch_n`, `egg_batch_min`, `egg_batch_max`):
the measured per-batch fecundity of *A. coluzzii* is **94–112 eggs per
female per gonotrophic cycle**, and the measured adult lifespan of a
resistant coluzzii female is **13.9 days** (susceptible 21.7 days), i.e.
consistent with the engine's adult daily mortality band (p ≈ 0.87–0.93 ⇒
mean lifespan 7.7–14.3 days).
**Caveats:** laboratory colonies from Yaoundé (Cameroon), not Ghana; the
resistant/susceptible contrast confounds genotype with colony history; the
susceptible colony had been maintained ~14 years in the insectarium.
